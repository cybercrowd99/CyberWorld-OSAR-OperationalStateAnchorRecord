# CyberWorld-OSAR V1 — OSARAnchor

## Purpose

OSARAnchor defines the controlled attachment mechanism of CyberWorld-OSAR.

The anchor connects a represented operational state to a specific operational target while preventing uncontrolled expansion of that state.

OSARAnchor answers:

> Where is this represented operational state attached?

OSARAnchor is an attachment mechanism.

OSARAnchor does not create:

- ownership
- identity
- authority
- unrestricted enforcement
- permanent classification
- world state control

OSARAnchor creates controlled containment only.

---

# OSARAnchor Doctrine

The anchor is not the object.

The anchor is not the authority.

The anchor is not the state.

The anchor is the containment point connecting a represented operational condition to a declared operational target.

Flow:

State

↓

OSARAnchor

↓

Operational Attachment

The represented condition remains separate from the object it references.

---

# Anchor Principle

The anchor exists to prevent operational bleed.

A represented condition remains attached only to its declared operational boundary.

The anchor never owns the object.

The anchor never becomes the authority.

The anchor preserves containment.

---

# OSARAnchor Structure

## anchorReference

Identifies the anchor instance.

Responsibilities:

- anchor retrieval
- lineage tracking
- validation support
- continuity preservation

The anchor reference identifies the containment point.

---

## attachedObject

Defines the operational object receiving the represented condition.

Examples:

- account
- lane
- capability
- process
- asset
- resource

The attached object remains independently owned.

---

## scopeReference

Defines where the anchor applies.

Responsibilities:

- operational boundary
- interpretation range
- containment limit

Scope prevents uncontrolled propagation.

---

## authorityReference

Links the anchor to AuthorityRef.

Authority remains external.

The anchor does not create authority.

---

## evidenceReference

Links supporting evidence.

Evidence remains external.

The anchor references evidence only.

---

## lifecycleReference

Links to OSARAnchorLifecycle.

Lifecycle controls movement.

The anchor cannot bypass lifecycle.

---

## createdAt

Defines anchor deployment time.

Clock Law applies.

---

## expiresAt

Defines anchor expiration time.

Anchors cannot exist indefinitely without renewal.

# OSARAnchor Boundary Rules

## No Ownership Creation

The anchor cannot transfer ownership.

The attached object remains with its original owner.

---

## No Identity Creation

The anchor cannot convert an operational condition into identity.

---

## No Authority Creation

Authority originates from AuthorityRef.

The anchor references authority.

---

## No Scope Expansion

The anchor cannot exceed declared scope.

No-Bleed Law applies.

---

## No Lifecycle Override

The anchor cannot bypass:

- lifecycle
- validation
- Clock Law
- registry controls
